1. What is the title of your program? 
    
    Belly Wally Lite

2. What real-world problem will your program address or help to solve?

    Belly Wally Lite will help campus food vendors manage student 
    meal subscriptions. Many vendors track customer meals, payments,
    and balances manually using notebooks or memory, which can lead to 
    mistakes and poor record keeping. This program will help vendors
    register customers, calculate meal costs, record deposits, 
    and track remaining balances more accurately.

    The program will also provide a summary of customer subscription
    and payment information in a simple and organized format.

3. What will you learn from developing this program?

    I will learn how to organize a larger Python project using 
    multiple functions that each perform a single task. I will strengthen
    my understanding of functions, parameters, return values, 
    decision structures, data validation, and testing with pytest. I will 
    also gain experience solving a real-world business problem using Python.

4. What Python modules will your program use?

    - pytest

    - math (if needed for calculations)

    I may also use built-in Python modules and functions for user input, 
    calculations, and data management.
